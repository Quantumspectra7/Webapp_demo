# Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)
## Operational Guidelines (Scheme Version 2.0 - Active 2022-2027)

### 1. Scheme Overview
The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) is a flagship credit-linked subsidy scheme administered by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MoMSME), Government of India, through the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) as the national nodal agency. At the state and district level in Punjab, the scheme is implemented through the Punjab KVIC Directorate, Punjab Khadi and Village Industries Board (PKVIB), and District Industries Centres (DICs).

### 2. Quantum and Nature of Financial Assistance
Under the updated May 2022 guidelines:
- **Maximum Project Cost**:
  - Manufacturing Sector: Up to ₹50.00 Lakhs
  - Service / Business Sector: Up to ₹20.00 Lakhs
- **Subsidy (Margin Money) Slabs**:
  - **Rural Areas**:
    - General Category: 25% margin money subsidy.
    - Special Category (SC, ST, OBC, Minorities, Women, Ex-Servicemen, Differently-Abled): 35% margin money subsidy.
  - **Urban Areas**:
    - General Category: 15% margin money subsidy.
    - Special Category: 25% margin money subsidy.
- **Beneficiary Own Contribution**:
  - General Category: 10% of total project cost.
  - Special Category: 5% of total project cost.

### 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Any individual above 18 years of age.
- No income ceiling for setting up projects under PMEGP.
- **Educational Qualification**: For setting up manufacturing units costing above ₹10 Lakhs, or service units costing above ₹5 Lakhs, the beneficiary must possess at least Standard VIII pass certificate.
- Assistance is strictly available only for **Greenfield** (newly established) units. Existing units are not eligible for first assistance.

### 4. Financing Bank Norms
- Participating financial institutions include all Scheduled Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks (such as Punjab Gramin Bank), and State Cooperative Banks.
- Loan Structure: Composite term loan and working capital limit.
- Collateral: No third-party collateral or mortgage required for loans up to ₹2.00 Crores under Credit Guarantee Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) coverage.
