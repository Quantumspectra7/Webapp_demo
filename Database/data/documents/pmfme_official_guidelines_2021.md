# PM Formalisation of Micro food processing Enterprises (PMFME) Scheme
## Guidelines for Individual Enterprises (Ministry of Food Processing Industries, GoI)

### 1. Scheme Overview
Launched under the Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan, PMFME is a centrally sponsored scheme designed to enhance the competitiveness of existing and new individual micro-enterprises in the unorganized segments of the food processing industry, promote formalization, and support Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Self Help Groups (SHGs), and Producer Cooperatives.

### 2. Credit-Linked Capital Subsidy Norms
- **Individual Micro Food Processing Units**:
  - Eligible micro-enterprises can avail a credit-linked capital subsidy of **35% of eligible project cost**, with a maximum ceiling of **₹10.00 Lakh per unit**.
  - The beneficiary's own contribution must be a minimum of **10% of the project cost**.
  - The remaining 55% to 90% is financed via commercial term loans through participating financial institutions.
- **Eligible Costs**:
  - Plant & machinery, food-grade processing equipment, sanitary civil alterations, testing instrumentation, and trial production working capital margin.
  - Cost of land is strictly excluded from eligible project cost calculations.

### 3. One District One Product (ODOP) Alignment
- In Punjab, priority sanction is accorded to enterprises working in designated ODOP clusters (e.g., Bakery and Confectionery/Wheat Processing in Ludhiana; Tomato/Veg Processing in Amritsar; Honey & Oilseeds in Bathinda).
- Non-ODOP agro-processing units are also eligible for micro upgradation when showing viable commercial demand.

### 4. Technical and Capacity Support
- Free technical training on food safety standards (FSSAI compliance), packaging technology, barcode onboarding, and GST formalization through District Resource Persons (DRPs) appointed in every district of Punjab.
