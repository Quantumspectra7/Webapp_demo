# Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF)
## Operational Guidelines (Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, GoI)

### 1. Scheme Context and Objective
The Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund (AHIDF) is established with a corpus of ₹15,000 Crore to incentivize investments by individual entrepreneurs, MSMEs, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Section 8 companies, and private limited entities in dairy processing, value-addition infrastructure, meat processing, and animal feed plants.

### 2. Key Financial Concessions
- **Interest Subvention**:
  - Government of India provides an **interest subvention of 3.0% per annum** on scheduled bank term loans.
  - Subvention is valid for the entire repayment tenure (up to 8 years including up to 2 years moratorium).
- **Loan Quantum and Equity Margin**:
  - Scheduled banks can finance up to **90% of the total project cost**.
  - Minimum equity contribution:
    - Micro and Small Enterprises: Minimum 10%
    - Medium Enterprises: Minimum 15%
    - Other categories: Minimum 25%
- **Credit Guarantee Support**:
  - Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Animal Husbandry (CGFTAH), managed by NABSanrakshan (NABARD subsidiary), provides credit guarantee coverage of up to 25% of the total credit facility for projects up to ₹50 Crore.

### 3. Eligible Activities in Punjab Focus
- Setting up of Bulk Milk Coolers (BMC) and rapid chilling nodes at the village cluster level.
- Value addition plants for manufacturing paneer, ghee, curd, flavored milk, and sterilized dairy beverages.
- Fully automated total mixed ration (TMR) and cattle feed pellet formulation mills.
